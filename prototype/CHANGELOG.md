# Homepage conversion prototype — before and after

## Before

- Large hero did not make the first action visible on the initial desktop view.
- Broad capability language required visitors to infer the practical deliverable.
- Proof did not consistently distinguish working software, prototypes, and research.
- The contact path was mailto-only with no visible project-intake sequence.

## After

- Hero now leads with **“Intelligent Software. Practical Business Results.”** and presents both requested actions above the fold.
- Service cards name concrete outputs: applications/websites, supervised automation, and data/ML systems.
- Proof uses actual Quant-AI screenshots, labeled as working research demonstration/mobile view; health and workflow items are labeled prototype/research with explicit boundaries.
- Contact is an accessible, non-transmitting prototype form with a three-step human-reviewed inquiry workflow.
- Navigation, colors, and an editorial blue/white visual system are preserved in a responsive static prototype.

## Safety and publication boundary

- This is a local prototype only. It does not change WordPress, DNS, hosting, production credentials, analytics, forms, or paid services.
- No Jackson Ranch information, customer claim, clinical-deployment claim, or stock imagery is included.
- The form intentionally does not submit or store data.
